Cool the cart, not the room

A traditional final holding cooler chills a whole panelled room so that the carts inside it stay cold. You pay to refrigerate the air, the walls, the floor and the doorway — and every time the doors open, you pay again.

Dispatch Chiller Rail applies the same principle as the vehicle system: filtered refrigerated air is forced directly into each cart through cart-operated dampers. The room around it can stay at ambient.

That removes the chiller walls, the ceiling, and the floor-space sizing constraint that dictates how big a dispatch cooler has to be.

Advantages

  • Reduces operating cost by more than 85%
  • Cool each flight only as required, cutting wasted energy further
  • Retrofits into an existing cooler, or stands alone where there is no cold room at all
  • Removes the floor-space sizing constraint of a traditional dispatch cooler
  • Position an entire flight together
  • Easily removed and re-installed when relocating a facility
  • Temperature records captured by flight, without user interaction
  • Enables preloading, increasing effective dispatch cooler capacity

Also installed in

The same principle has been extended beyond the dispatch cooler:

  • Loading docks
  • Extensions to existing dispatch chillers
  • Pre-chilling in bar-pack areas
