Dispatch Chiller Rail

Patent pending

A final holding cooler without the cooler. Fit it into existing space, cool only the flights you are dispatching, and take it with you if you move.

80% Minimum reduction in operating cost
$648 Saved per day on a medium flight kitchen cooler
$0.09 Per amp, the basis of the calculation

Figures based on a medium-sized flight kitchen final holding cooler at $0.09 per amp: 0.09 × 100 amps × 3 phases × 24 hours.

Advantages

  • Reduces operating costs significantly — at least 80%
  • Cool each flight as required, further reducing wasted energy
  • Can be retrofitted into an existing cooler
  • Removes the need for a traditional dispatch cooler and its floor-space sizing constraints
  • Able to position an entire flight together
  • Easily removed when relocating a facility
  • Effective temperature record keeping by flight, without user interaction
