How Chiller Rail works

Patent pending

The cart becomes the cooler. That single change is what removes the panelling, the sizing constraints and most of the running cost.

The air path

A plenum generates cold air, which is forced to each airline cart through lightweight HEPA or UV-filtered ducts. Refrigerated air enters the cart at constant pressure and returns through the lower vent.

By exploiting the properties of the airline cart itself, the cart becomes the cooled volume. Product temperature is maintained without panelling the vehicle or the dispatch cooler.

Ovens, canisters and any carts without vents are loaded into a purpose-designed area so they chill correctly alongside the rail.

Installation and scale

Chiller Rail fits quickly within an existing cooler, or stands alone in a facility that has no traditional panelled cold room at all.

Because the system scales to match the volume actually being cooled, cost falls further again — you cool the flights you are dispatching, not the whole building.

Temperature records

Internal cart temperature is recorded wirelessly to a central system. Records are captured by flight without anyone having to write anything down, which removes both the labour and the gap in the audit trail.
