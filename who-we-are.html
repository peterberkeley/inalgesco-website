How we work

Inalgesco exists because the conventional answer to keeping catering cold — build a bigger insulated box, or throw dry ice at the problem — is expensive to run, slow to install, and inflexible once it is in.

We start from the equipment that is already in the operation. The airline cart is a well-engineered container that nobody was using as part of the cooling system. Chiller Rail does exactly that, which is why it retrofits in a day rather than a shutdown.
