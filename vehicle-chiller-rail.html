Vehicle Chiller Rail

Patent pending

Retrofit any high loader in a day and carry carts under refrigeration — without the weight of a traditional system, and without dry ice.

1 day Retrofit time per high loader
No dry ice Recurring cost removed entirely
Reusable Remove and refit when a vehicle reaches end of life

Advantages

  • Reliable and robust fleet refrigeration
  • Temperature performance recording with no driver input
  • Clean HEPA and UV system, maintaining a food-safe environment
  • Retrofits to an existing fleet with minimal downtime
  • Eliminates dry ice costs, generating a fast return on investment
  • System can be removed and refitted when a vehicle reaches end of life
  • Limited user training required
  • Lightweight, with no significant effect on vehicle payload
  • Vehicles can be preloaded
  • Substantially lower CO₂ emissions than traditional refrigeration
  • Creates competitive advantage
