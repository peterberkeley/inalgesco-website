Evidence

Every claim on this site traces back to field inspection, manufacturer service data or third-party quotations. Here is the working.

Field audit — Florida, three stations

  • 42 vehicles inspected across Orlando, Tampa and Miami
  • 36 fitted with factory Carrier 850 diesel refrigeration
  • Zero working refrigeration systems found, in all three units
  • Two high loaders aged 2–4 years: no operational system, repair quoted at $16,000–$20,000 each
  • Typical run time 10 hours per day; Carrier 850 rated at 1.25 gal/hr — around $22,800 of fuel per vehicle per year

Sources: Efficiency Maintenance (Gate Gourmet mechanic, Orlando); Carrier commercial service centres, Orlando and Tampa.

The four saving streams

Per truck, per year, on a high loader running twelve hours a day.

  • Fuel — the diesel reefer engine is eliminated. Carrier 850 rated 1.25 gal/hr at manufacturer spec
  • Maintenance — the full Carrier service schedule (Services A–F, three-monthly to annual) goes away. Scheduled parts alone run about $8,700/yr, before labour
  • Dry ice — active refrigeration removes the requirement throughout the run
  • Off-road disruption — engine-driven units need roughly four days off-road per major service event. No engine, no combustion components, no exposure

The four streams net out near $23,800 per truck per year.

Reliability, side by side

Carrier 850

  • Two-stroke diesel engine, roughly 10 gallons per day
  • Driven through a complex belt and pulley system
  • Start-stop vehicle use makes failure routine
  • High-pressure refrigerant gas, outlawed in Europe in 2004
  • Sized to cool 2,300 cu ft of cargo space
  • Runs at full power whenever it runs
  • Needs the cargo doors shut — which they rarely are

ChillRail

  • Electrically driven from excess vehicle power
  • Under 50% capacity at startup, then around 15%
  • Low-pressure system, greenhouse-friendly refrigerant
  • No external moving parts; two internal digital fans rated 5,000 hrs
  • Parts warranted, readily available, fittable by local fleet maintenance
  • Cloud monitoring flags faults early
  • Runs only when needed, shuts down when not
