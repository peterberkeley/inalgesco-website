Better, quicker, cheaper, more reliable.

Traditional cold chain treats the room as the cooler. We treat the cart as the cooler.

A plenum generates cold air and forces it through lightweight HEPA or UV-filtered ducting into each cart at constant pressure, returning through the lower vent. The cart’s own construction does the rest — which means no panelled walls, no ceiling work, and no fixed sizing constraints.

Both systems are patent pending and retrofit to existing equipment.

Two systems

One approach, applied either side of the dispatch door.

Dispatch Chiller Rail

Turns unrefrigerated warehouse space into a final holding cooler without building chiller walls or ceilings. Cool each flight only as required, position a whole flight together, and scale to the size of the operation.

Vehicle Chiller Rail

Lets unrefrigerated vehicles carry airline carts under refrigeration. Retrofits to an existing fleet with minimal downtime, adds no meaningful payload weight, and removes dry ice from the operation entirely.
